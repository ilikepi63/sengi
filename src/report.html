    Input schema 
    
      
    
      
    Parts of the input and how each is measured
    
      
    Each part's share is how much of all the differences between the inputs comes from it, including everything below it. A part that varies by itself lists its parameters: blue are distances, which must reach their threshold to make two inputs different (above 1 they never decide alone); orange are categories, covered pairwise. Click ▾ to fold a part.
    
      
    
    
    
  
    

  
    
    
    
      
    Distance between every pair
    
      
    Each cell is the weighted mean distance between two inputs, in order. Light cells away from the diagonal are near-duplicates.
    
      
    0 same1 completely different
    
      
    
    
    

    
    
      
    Corpus map
    
      
    Inputs placed so that the distance between dots approximates the distance between inputs. The axes have no units. An even spread means a well-spread corpus. Inputs that are equally far from everything else land on the same spot and overlap.
    
      
    
      
    
    
    
  
    

  
    
    
    What makes inputs different
    
    
    Share of all differences between all pairs of inputs, following the structure of the input from the top row down to the parameters at the bottom of each column. A distance parameter counts its weight × distance; a category counts its weight whenever two inputs fall in different categories. Hover for details.

    Growth
    
    
    Corpus size against candidates generated. A long flat tail means new candidates stopped adding anything.
    
    
    
  
    

  
    
    
    Near-duplicates
    
    
    Pairs of inputs with the same categories that aren't clearly different on any distance at θ: they exercise the same thing, and one of each pair could go. Each lists the parameters that came closest to telling them apart, as distance / threshold.
